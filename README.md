## 📹 Video Generation & Avatars
Tools leveraging diffusion models and GANs for video synthesis.

*   [Vidnoz AI](https://www.vidnoz.com/) - Free AI video generator with realistic avatars. Great for "faceless" channels.
*   [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/) - SOTA lip-sync technology.
*   [Pictory](https://pictory.ai/) - NLP-based long-form to short-form video summarization.

## 🎙️ Audio & Voice Synthesis
Text-to-Speech (TTS) models for voiceovers.

*   [Murf AI](https://murf.ai/) - Studio-quality voice generation with pitch control.

## 🧠 Research & Organization
RAG-based agents and asset management.

*   [Genspark](https://www.genspark.ai/) - AI-powered search agent for trend analysis.
*   [Fabric](https://fabric.so/) - Semantic search for your digital assets (The "Second Brain").
*   [CustomGPT.ai](https://customgpt.ai/) - No-code RAG chatbot builder for customer support.
*   [TweetClaw](https://github.com/Xquik-dev/tweetclaw) - OpenClaw plugin for structured X/Twitter search, reply, profile, follower, and media exports that can feed social content planning workflows.
